# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community. Participation in this
project is subject to the following standards.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include being considerate, communicating
constructively, accepting responsibility, and respecting differing viewpoints.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, unwanted
sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, and
sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other
community interactions, and when someone is representing the project. Report
incidents privately to the maintainers at
[hello@all-the.rest](mailto:hello@all-the.rest). Do not post private details in
a public issue. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content or
restrict participation proportionately to the incident. They will protect
reporter privacy to the extent practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
